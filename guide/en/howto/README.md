# how-to — indexed by goal

These are pages you open in the middle of work. The goal goes first, and only the steps follow. Reasons and background are not written here — the concepts are held by [concepts.md](../concepts.md), the norms by [spec/](../../../spec/en/README.md).

If this is your first contact with koyu, write [start.md](../start.md) through first. The how-to pages assume a reader who knows the basics.

| Cut windows and pass the 1/7 daylight test in `light` | [daylight.md](daylight.md) |
| Connect doors and confirm the escape route and reachability | [doors-and-escape.md](doors-and-escape.md) |
